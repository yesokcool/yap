Get Yap

Yap runs right in your browser. The desktop app adds push-to-talk that works while you're in a game, and it's tiny: no Electron.

First launch
  1. Windows: SmartScreen may warn about an unknown app. Click More info › Run anyway.
  2. Linux: chmod +x yap-*-linux-x86_64, then run it.
  3. Mac: in Terminal, chmod +x yap-*-macos-aarch64 && xattr -d com.apple.quarantine yap-*-macos-aarch64, then run it.
  4. Sign on with your screen name and PIN. After that the app updates itself: when a new version is out, click Update in its status bar.
iPhone and iPad

The iPhone app is on its way through TestFlight. Until then, open Yap in Safari and choose Share › Add to Home Screen.
